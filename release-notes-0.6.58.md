# Kiki 0.6.58

- Choose Light, Dark, or Follow System in Settings → General → Appearance. Your choice is saved and applies immediately across the workspace and listening display. Dark remains the default.
- Light mode uses a quiet sage-neutral palette with readable text, native light controls, and coordinated sidebar, cards, tables, and menus.
- Standardized action-button typography and heights, widened model actions for their labels, and kept hover feedback immediate.
- Added steady-width numeric labels for timers, dates, and setup steps; widened history dates and refined nested corners and keyboard focus outlines.
