# Kiki 0.6.56

- Adds a **Create Summary** action after meeting transcription. Kiki creates a private on-device brief with a summary, key points, and explicit next steps; the result remains editable and is included in Markdown exports.
- Gives completed meetings their own **Meetings** library instead of mixing them into everyday dictation history.
- Bounds routine dictation history to the most recent 250 entries by default. The Privacy page can keep 50, 250, 1,000, or retain entries until they are deleted; meetings and imported-audio transcripts remain until explicitly removed.
