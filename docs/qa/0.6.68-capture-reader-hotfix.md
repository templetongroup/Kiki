# Capture Meeting reader hotfix

Isolated from the unapproved summary-model experiments, based on published 0.6.67 source.

## Reproduction

The actual `MeetingWindowController` diagnostic failed before the repair:

```text
FAIL Blank capture reader: document=(0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 15452.0)
container=Optional((-28.0, 10000000.0)) viewport=Optional((902.0, 239.0))
```

A simplified standalone NSTextView test passed and was discarded: it did not reproduce the production layout. The controller diagnostic is the regression seam.

## Repair and checks

- Capture Meeting now uses the same width-tracking `TranscriptReaderView` as the library.
- `bash scripts/test-capture-reader.sh` exercises the actual controller, detaches and embeds its content as the workbench does, loads 959 synthetic lines, resizes to three widths, and checks scrolling to both ends. It also renders a native view image for visual inspection.
- After repair, document widths were 902, 762, and 1142 points, matching the respective scroll view widths. Text was visible in the rendered view.
- The existing library reader regression passes.
- Summary failures now throw an explicit error instead of silently saving the first two utterances as a successful summary. This does not establish improved summarization quality. Existing saved content is not rewritten.
- The packaging command disables manifest caching because the manifest detects an optional locally supplied Sparkle binary; stale evaluation otherwise kept attempting a remote download.

The optional diagnostic history-file and record-ID arguments read saved content without modifying history. Private meeting inputs and screenshots must stay outside Git.

## Scope

This hotfix does not include experimental model selection, capture/transcription changes, or a claim of summary accuracy. Installed-device verification and distribution status must be recorded separately after execution.

## Installed-device result — September 29

Developer ID signed 0.6.68 (101) was accepted by Apple notarization (submission `f1355385-0b19-41da-9848-158778973944`), stapled, and accepted by Gatekeeper on both the build Mac and Work MBP. The prior Work MBP app was retained in its private recovery folder before replacement.

The diagnostic was rerun from `/Applications/Kiki.app` on the Work MBP against today's actual saved meeting. All three widths passed, including scrolling to both ends; document heights were 34,812, 36,556, and 33,100 points. The native rendered view visibly contains the saved meeting. Its history checksum matched the pre-install backup after testing. No transcript or existing summary was rewritten. Kiki was relaunched normally after the diagnostic.

This is a directly installed hotfix, not a published Sparkle/GitHub/landing-page release. Summary generation quality remains unresolved; the new behavior reports failure rather than substituting opening fragments. A current meeting's live in-memory Capture tab is not restored on relaunch; its saved record remains in Meetings.
