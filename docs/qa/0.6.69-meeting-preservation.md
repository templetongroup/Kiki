# 0.6.69 meeting preservation hotfix

## Reproduced defect

The final meeting path called the normal dictation processor. Its Polished Speech mode discards text before a correction phrase; snippets and personalized replacements also alter the result. The affected test Mac has Polished Speech enabled. This establishes an enabled defect, not how many words it removed from any earlier meeting without the recording.

`bash scripts/test-meeting-post-processing.sh` failed against the original processing path. After the repair it passes for every profile: meeting speech, negation, corrections and original wording survive, snippet-like meeting speech is not expanded, and normal dictation editing still works.

## Release scope

Only meeting post-processing isolation and the previously tested Whisper GPU-resource packaging repair are included. Experimental local summarizers, boundary-context changes and other work on the summary-reliability branch are excluded. No existing transcript or summary is rewritten.
